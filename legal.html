Privacy

This website does not collect personal data.

  • No forms. You don’t enter any information on this site.

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you contact us. If you message us on WhatsApp, by email or on Instagram, that service handles your message under its own privacy policy. We use your name, contact details and what you tell us only to reply and to discuss your project. We don’t sell or share them. You can ask us at any time to delete your messages and details.
